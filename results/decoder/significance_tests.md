## Power with n=3

Minimum effect size detectable with 80% power (two-sided, alpha=0.05), from the noncentral
t distribution.

| n | Unpaired d | Paired d_z |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | 3.07 | 3.26 |
| 6 | 1.80 | 1.43 |
| 10 | 1.32 | 1.00 |
| 20 | 0.91 | 0.66 |

With 3 seeds per method the minimum is d = 3.07. With the median per-cell seed std of
0.0075 (GLUE tasks) and 0.0056 (free-form tasks), this is a gap of about
2.3 and 1.7 points. Smaller gaps cannot be confirmed. For a claim of
equivalence use a TOST with a stated margin.
